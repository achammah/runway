# Binder rework, part 2 — the remaining desks + retrofits

Companion to `11-binder-rework.md` (laws 1–7, hero grammar) and
`DECISIONS.md` (owner picks; binding). This file specs the desks that
did not get their own choose-round: the owner delegated them in the
established grammars. Every desk below obeys: the hero answers the
tab's question · money in columns · numbered didactic zones where
concepts are taught · the collapse ladder · audience/type variants
where truth differs · the mutation law (receipt → two-tap → Esc) ·
red climbs to the tab · generated vocabulary written once at run
start.

The binder is 18 desks + momentary tabs:
- REVENUE: offers · customers · in motion · growth
- COSTS: spend · team · recruitment · bills · the bank · the works
- THE COMPANY: what we make · cap table · the raise · the street ·
  threats · pivot (+ THE OFFER, momentary)
- THE LOG: this week · history · events

## the street — "what is the world doing to us?"

Zones:
1. **THE WEATHER** — the macro season as a drawn banner (winter:
   coral band + snow scribble, valuations ×0.6 named plainly; boom:
   sage sun). One sentence on what the season changes THIS week.
2. **THE RIVALS** — one card per rival: name big, posture words as
   chips (undercutting / hiring / wooing your accounts), heat dot,
   last-3 moves with week stamps. Collapse ladder: top 3 by threat +
   "the other N ▸"; a rival act that targeted YOU stays face-up.
3. **THE INVESTORS' MOOD** — what the market pays now (multiples
   band, appetite word), feeding THE RAISE's radar; cold market =
   fewer inbound knocks, said explicitly.
4. **TAKEN FROM US / THE WIRE** — poaches attempted, price wars
   active, disruptor watch — each row with its counter-desk jump.

## threats — the command center

- HERO: the loudest item BIG with its desk named ("no price on the
  wall — offers"), count of the rest under it.
- THE LIST: rows — severity dot, plain-words label, desk word as a
  pressable jump (`focus_desk`). Sorted by severity then age.
- Spillover strip stays. Teaching foot: "this same list is what THE
  PRE-ROLL REVIEW reads before any dice."
- No collapse: if the list is long, that IS the message.

## pivot — the escape hatch (mechanics in DECISIONS)

Zones:
1. **THE TWO DOORS** — two big cards: AUDIENCE PIVOT (customers → 0,
   market learning dies, product + cash + team survive) and PRODUCT
   PIVOT (customers take a 50–100% roll, product advances die,
   channel learning + cash survive). Each door lists its exact costs
   in the door's own words; debts survive both, said on both.
2. **THE PREVIEW** — computed, not asserted: N customers lost, the
   well's $X drained, N named deals dead, v0.62 → v0.1, which topics
   and paintings regenerate, what the price book rewrites.
3. **THE WEEK AFTER** — what the first post-pivot week looks like
   (demand ramp restarts, the DM narrates the pivot).
4. **THE ARM** — two-tap + the word PIVOT typed. Esc keeps the
   company. After the arm, the pivot resolves at the next LOCK IN.

## this week — the desk you play from

- HERO: week number + era + the event card (art slot) + one plain
  sentence of the week's situation.
- Zones: THE CARD (event, its bite) · YOUR MOVE (the written-move
  composer; clarify answers append after " — ") · ARMED THIS WEEK
  (receipt list of every change staged since the last roll — budget
  moves, offers changed, arranges, signatures — each with its desk
  jump) · LOCK IN (the die; THE PRE-ROLL REVIEW card intercepts when
  attention items exist).
- This desk is the default landing tab of the binder.

## history — the run's own ledger

- Ledger-sheet rows per week: wk · cash · net · customers · the
  headline ("the shark rolled up") · receipts ▸ (opens that week's
  full receipt list). Collapse by era (era sections with subtotals of
  net). Sparklines strip (cash, customers) above.
- Folded momentary tabs (answered buyout offers, closed rounds) file
  here as flagged rows.

## events — the mail

- The inbox stream: letters and notices (investor knock, employee
  ask, cliff coming, covenant warning, price-book quote arriving),
  newest first, unread bold, each with its desk jump and week stamp.
- Distinction from threats: threats = standing dangers ranked;
  events = the stream as it happened. An event that requires action
  also registers a threat until answered.
- Collapse: read items fold by week.

## Retrofits to locked picks (audience/density variants)

- **offers = RATE CARD**: Consumer runs price few offers at volume —
  the card shows units/wk per row; Enterprise adds a per-seat column
  and named-account discounts line (fed by pipeline).
- **customers = SCOREBOARD**: Consumer = counts + cohort retention
  bars; SMB = counts + a "biggest 5" strip; Enterprise = named logos
  grid with seats + renewal clocks.
- **group overviews = QUARTET**: each card is that page's hero
  verbatim (one number + one sentence + red if red); COSTS' quartet
  becomes a quintet/sextet grid as pages grew — same card grammar,
  grid wraps.
- **TEAM thread**: every row gains the vesting mini-bar when a grant
  exists ("0.4% · 31% vested · cliff wk 52 ▸ cap table").
- **the bank**: gains the REFINANCE row under the notes (mutation
  law) — swap a note for a new quote at today's standing, break fee
  shown on the receipt.

## Momentary desks (pattern)

Gold tab, slides into its group when its event fires, carries a
deadline clock, folds into HISTORY when resolved. First: THE OFFER
(spec in DECISIONS). The IPO window and a priced-round CLOSING
(signature ceremony: final cap table preview before wiring) reuse the
pattern later.
