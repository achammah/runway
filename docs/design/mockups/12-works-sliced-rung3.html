The Works, Sliced

RUNG 3, GENERALIZED · PICK ONE LETTER

Right — storefronts and maps assume shops on streets. The general truth of rung 3: at scale, the works splits along an axis that DIFFERS per business — studios for a spa, products for a SaaS, plants or SKUs for hardware, categories for a marketplace — and sometimes more than one axis at once. So the design hardcodes NOTHING about the axis: the axis name is generated content (written at run start like everything else), a "sliced by ▾" control regroups the same numbers along another axis (by offer, by customer type), and every division shows the SAME five facts — used%, volume, cost each, margin each, status — and opens into its own rung-2 works.

To prove there's no overfit, each option below renders TWICE: the massage chain (sliced by studios) and then a SaaS (sliced by products) on the identical skeleton. The centerpiece lesson generalizes with it: "same unit, different books" — Lyon $27 vs Geneva $36 of rent; a seat of Core $2.90 vs a seat of Legacy $9.40 of support minutes.
