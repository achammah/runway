The Works, Four Businesses

TAB 9 / 16 · A LOCKED + GENERALIZED · SAY OK OR ADJUST

Locked: A (the four-zone meeting) — renamed THE WORKS, because every business has one: the desk of everything it costs to DELIVER what you sell. A massage is a unit leaving a one-person factory; a seat of software is a unit served by servers and a care team; a matched order is a unit made of other people's shops. Same four questions everywhere — can we serve? · what does one cost? · what makes the capacity? · what's the relief valve? — answered in each business's own units and words (written once at run start, like the garden).

The honest differences are kept, not smoothed: software's gap is degradation and churn, not turned-away revenue; a marketplace's machines are other people's shops, with a concentration warning; a service's capacity sleeps when people do and reads straight from the team. Hardware stays as designed (presses, resale at half). The unit ticket is always the offer's generated cost lines — the engine already itemizes them.

Below: service (your massage example), software, marketplace — hardware is in the archive. Say OK (or what to adjust) — then THE COMPANY group opens with PRODUCT.
