Round three: your pick — the Divider Rail crossed with the Drawers, pushed to look like a real binder. Every option now has binder anatomy: a kraft cover, drawn rings, punched pages — and expandable divider groups: closed groups stay as labeled dividers carrying their live total and bangs; the open group fans out its pages. Same taxonomy everywhere (REVENUE · COSTS · THE COMPANY · THE LOG, 15 desks), same hero pages. Four ways to build the object.

What it wins

All groups always visible AND expanded pages — no divider ever hides. The spiral is cheap in pixels and instantly "notebook".

What it costs

Two rails (group + pages) spend width; vertical text on the group tabs is slower to read; the least "ring binder", the most "spiral pad".

Choose if

Navigation certainty beats the drawer/expansion play — everything visible, always.
