The Product Page, Ten Ways

TAB 10 / 16 · CORRECTED + TEN STYLES · PICK ONE (OR MIX)

Understood, and corrected: this tab is the product's own page — you see WHAT it is (name + what it does, up top in every option), WHAT IT'S MADE OF (the concrete features, each with its job — brings them in / keeps them / lets us charge / plumbing — its solidity and its keep-cost), THE WHOLE PIPELINE (shelf of priced ideas → next → building → ready → live, with the dice-at-press SHIP, the self-ship clock, the −25% stand-down), and WHAT THE PRODUCT COSTS (build + keep + what its features add to every unit's ticket). Debt stops being an abstract jar: it's the creaky feature you can point at.

All ten carry identical facts (a scheduling SaaS) so the comparison is pure style. Every option works for any business — the features of a spa are its service elements, of a device its components, of a marketplace its platform pieces:

spa: the 50-min protocol · hot stones · online booking · loyalty cardhardware: motor · shell · firmware · the appmarketplace: escrow · ratings · search · same-day courier
