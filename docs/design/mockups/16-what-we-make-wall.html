TAB 10 / 16 · THE WALL, EXPLORED · SAY OK OR ADJUST

The wall, taken seriously — and the tab renamed "WHAT WE MAKE", because "product" was too narrow: a spa makes an experience, a device maker makes the device, a marketplace makes the platform. (Prefer "the craft" or "the thing"? One word swaps it.) The trio is now clean: offers = what we charge for it · what we make = the thing itself · the works = what delivering it costs.

The page is two parts and takes its length: THE PIPELINE WALL on top — four columns, each with its meaning written under the header (shelf: priced ideas, press to commit · next: the queue, reorder freely · building: money burning against odds · ready: ship it or it ships itself) — and LIVE, THE INVENTORY as a full-width band below, grouped by job, one card per feature with its solidity dot, keep-cost, and the measured note on recent landings ("promised +price, measured +12%"). One card anatomy everywhere, so the wall reads as one system. The creaky card IS the debt — pointable, rebuildable.

Four businesses below on the identical wall — SaaS, a massage studio, a drone-kit maker, a marketplace. Only the vocabulary changes, and it's generated at run start. Say OK (or adjust) — then CAP TABLE.
