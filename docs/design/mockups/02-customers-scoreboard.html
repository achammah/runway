The first-open tour — decided

The first time the binder opens, six steps: one per section, one for the red, one to hand over. Click advances, Esc skips, never repeats.

1 · REVENUE — the section opens, its pages fan out, one line each:
"offers — what you sell and charge · customers — who pays · pipeline — big deals in motion · growth — how they find you"
2 · COSTS — same fan, one line per page: "spend — dollars you choose · team — people · bills — what arrives anyway · the bank — debt · the factory — hardware only"
3 · THE COMPANY — "product — what you're building · cap table — who owns it · the street — rivals · threats — what could kill you"
4 · THE LOG — "this week's receipts, the whole history, the clocks that are ticking"
5 · THE RED — a mock tab flashes alarm red: "when a tab looks like THIS, it needs a decision — the ! climbs onto closed sections so nothing hides."
6 · YOURS — "that's the whole binder. Esc closes it; TAB reopens it. The week won't roll while something red is unanswered — unless you tell it to."

Mechanics: dimmed ink wash over everything but the focused element · runs once per install (its own marker) · replayable from the how-to screen.
