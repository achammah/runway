The Ledger Sheet

ONE ACCOUNTING PRIMITIVE · THREE DESKS RE-RENDERED · SAY OK OR ADJUST

Bills = B, rebuilt as a real book of accounts — and since spend and team are the same family, all three now share one primitive: THE LEDGER SHEET. Paper book-keeping, not a webpage table: fine column rules, faint row numbers, the amount column carries the classic green ledger band, small-caps header states the unit once. The accounting rules are law: single rule above a subtotal, DOUBLE RULE above the total, total at the bottom always, and the book must balance to the hero's number. Sections fold to their subtotal (the collapse ladder); the total row never scrolls away.

Below: bills, then spend and team re-rendered on the same sheet. Say OK (or what to adjust) — then THE BANK round, whose BOOKS mode reuses this sheet for the full P&L statement.
