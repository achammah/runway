# THE QUIET LAW — restraint for the binder (owner: "not award winning,
# way too busy")

The UX systems each earned their place; their SUM is noise. Every desk
passed its own gate — nobody gated the total ink on a pane. These rules
are enforced IN THE KIT so quiet is structural, not aspirational.

**R1 · ONE FOCAL POINT.** The hero is the only large object on a pane.
Everything else is supporting matter at supporting weight. If two
things compete for the first glance, one of them is wrong.

**R2 · THE ANNOTATION BUDGET.** Beyond the tab's own red, a pane may
carry at most THREE attention annotations: the ask strip (when red),
the hero delta, and ONE more (the single worst changed row). The kit
arbitrates: marks REGISTER with a priority instead of drawing
directly; the kit renders the top three and silently drops the rest.
A page where everything is marked has marked nothing.

**R3 · THE GUTTER DOT replaces the pen circle.** Changed rows get a
small 6px sage/coral dot in the LEFT GUTTER — never an ellipse drawn
around content (the circle fights the row's own borders and doubles
the ink). The drawn circle is reserved for the spotlight/tour only.

**R4 · ONE DELTA PER PANE.** The hero's ▲/▼ is the only delta glyph.
Memos, ratios, secondary numbers: plain text; their change is carried
by the gutter dot if it matters, by nothing if it doesn't.

**R5 · THE SLOT GRID.** Fixed vertical slots, identical on every desk:
hero 6–96 · ask strip 96–118 (drawn only when red; content NEVER
creeps into the slot when it is empty — stability beats density) ·
content 126 to DO_LANE_Y−8 · the DO lane · the teaching foot. Nothing
renders in another slot. Deep stacks fold rather than slide the foot.

**R6 · THE RED SINGLETON.** At most ONE red text object per pane — the
ask strip. Red zone labels, red warnings and red footers demote to
coral or fold their words INTO the strip. Row-level alarm clock chips
stay (they are data, not commentary). Red remains the loudest thing on
the page precisely because it appears once.

**R7 · THE AIR FLOOR.** ≥10px between any two text objects; row pitch
≥22px; duplicate captions die (the hero question line is suppressed
when the teaching foot already says it; a micro-status shown on the
rail is not repeated on the page). When a pane feels tight, delete a
line — never shrink one.

**R8 · TYPE DISCIPLINE.** Three sizes per pane, from the established
scale. The systems pass introduced 11–15px strays (chips, badges,
statuses) — snap them to the scale's detail size or the chip size,
nothing in between.

**R9 · KNOWN OVERLAPS, killed in the kit.** The crumb and the back
pill share the top-left — give them one slot (crumb first, pill after,
fixed gap). capbars' pct label clears the end tick at 100%. The works'
deep stacks fold instead of riding the foot below its slot.
hero_question yields on panes whose foot carries the question.

**R10 · THE GRADE GATE.** The polish probe COUNTS annotation objects
per pane and fails any pane over budget; every desk is re-shot and
eyeballed against one question — "would a design juror flinch?" —
before the pass closes.
